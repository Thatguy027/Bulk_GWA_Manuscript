INTERPRETATION
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  The PURE CHARGE EFFECT (Analysis 1) shows the direct electrostatic
  consequence of T->K substitution at position 96 on the lumenal face:
    Local potential shift near res96: +0.0271 a.u.
  A positive value indicates that T96K increases the positive
  electrostatic potential in the dsRNA-binding region, consistent
  with enhanced attraction to the dsRNA phosphate backbone.

  The CONFORMATIONAL COMPARISON (Analysis 2) additionally captures
  backbone rearrangements between the two AF3 models. Differences
  from Analysis 1 may reflect genuine structural accommodation of
  the Lys side chain or AF3 modelling noise.

  Res96 z-coordinates (lumenal face, +z):
    WT chain A: +62.2 A  |  T96K chain A: +68.8 A
    Both structures position residue 96 well above the bilayer
    on the dsRNA-accessible lumenal surface.
